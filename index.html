About Me

Hello! I'm Kevin Lewis, a current Master's student at Georgia Tech. I study computer science, and I am interested in a wide variety of CS topics, particularly programming languages and formal verification. I will be graduating in May of 2027, after which I am hoping to pursue a PhD and eventually a career in academia, where I can combine my love of computers with my love of teaching.


In my undergrad at Ohio State, I majored in both computer science and mathematics. My interest in fields like algebra and number theory inform how I tackle formal verification, as in many cases, modifying our theoretical representations of data can allow us to exploit properties that make verification much more tractable.
